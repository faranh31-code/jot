Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 25, 2026 · Last Updated: July 25, 2026

Jot ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Jot mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Jot is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

  • User-Generated Content: Any text, headlines, or data you create within the App are stored locally on your device and synced to Firebase. This data is tied to a device-generated anonymous ID, not your personal identity.
  • Saved Entries: Headlines and content you save are stored in Firebase Firestore and associated with your anonymous device ID.
  • Purchase Information: If you subscribe to Jot Pro, payment processing is handled by Apple/Google and managed by RevenueCat. We do not store or have access to your payment details.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Photos or media from your device
  • Contacts or social connections
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising ID, and interaction data to serve ads. For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Used to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat processes purchase receipts and manages subscriber status. For more information, visit RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase: Used for cloud storage and syncing your entries across devices. Firebase stores your anonymous ID and text entries. For more information, visit Firebase's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo: Used for app development and build infrastructure. Expo does not collect user data through the App itself. For more information, visit Expo's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

Jot displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob. AdMob may use cookies, device identifiers, and advertising IDs to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → opt out of Ads Personalization

5. In-App Purchases

Jot offers a free tier and a "Jot Pro" subscription. All payment transactions are processed by Apple/Google through the App Store/Play Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card information, billing address, or any payment credentials. RevenueCat manages subscription status on our behalf.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your text entries are stored in Firebase Firestore with an anonymous device ID. This data is encrypted in transit and at rest. If you uninstall the App, your locally stored data will be permanently deleted. Firebase cloud data may persist until manually removed.

7. Children's Privacy

Jot is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
